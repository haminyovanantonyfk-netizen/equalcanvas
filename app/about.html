A diagram is more than a picture.

Follow its connections. Understand the next step. Make a change and share the same file back.

EqualCanvas is a browser app that pairs keyboard-operated relationship controls with a visual diagram. It aims to help blind, low-vision, and sighted collaborators work with shared .drawio files.

Open the free editor →

No account, subscription, paid feature tier, or API key. Works offline after the first successful load. Install from a compatible browser on your phone or laptop.

From a blank page to a connected idea

Build with words

Turn a list into up to 100 connected steps. Start with a project, support process, or learning plan. Add the next step and its connection together.

Explore relationships

Follow incoming and outgoing connections, browse groups, find a directed route, and search actions, pages, or steps with Ctrl/Command+K.

Organize and explain

Create, rename, duplicate, reorder, and delete pages with undo. Export a readable text outline of every page and connection.

Notice loose ends

Review broken endpoints, unclear branch labels, disconnected steps, and loops. Jump to the relevant item. A loop may be intentional; checks are observations.

Your files stay on your device

The app processes diagrams in memory. It has no upload service, trackers, analytics, or cloud account. The offline cache stores only public app files. Download your work before closing or reloading: diagrams are not automatically saved.

The website host receives ordinary requests when you load the app. GitHub links open an external service; diagram contents are never included.

Help make participation easier

This is an early working release, with automated accessibility checks. It still needs evaluation with blind participants and real screen readers. Complex shapes and rich formatting are simplified in the preview.

Try the built-in example, tell us where navigation becomes confusing, or contribute a fix. Please use a synthetic example instead of sharing private diagrams.
