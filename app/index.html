        Keyboard & screen readers

        • Tab moves between controls.
        • ↑ / ↓ moves through the step list; Home / End jumps to its ends. Enter selects the focused step.
        • Ctrl / ⌘ + S downloads your diagram.
        • Ctrl / ⌘ + Z undoes a diagram change. Add Shift to redo. Text fields keep their normal editing shortcuts.
        • Escape closes a dialog.

        Your files belong to you

        Diagram contents stay in browser memory. They are not uploaded or automatically saved. Download your work before closing this tab. Offline caching stores only the app and its public example.

        First release boundaries

        Basic steps, decisions, groups, directed connections, and multiple pages are supported. Images, custom shapes, edge decorations, and rich formatting are simplified in this preview. Connection navigation follows the file’s source-to-target direction. Use draw.io to inspect complex visual layouts.

        Files are limited to 8 MB, 40 pages, 2,000 cells per page, and 24 MB of combined expanded page data. This release has automated accessibility checks; it has not yet been validated with blind participants.

        To install, use your browser’s “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” option when available. HTTPS or localhost is required for offline mode.
