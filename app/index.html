              Your files belong to you
              
      
              
        Diagram contents stay in browser memory. They are not uploaded or
        automatically saved. Download your work before closing this tab. Offline
        caching stores only the app and its public example.
      
              
      
              Build and manage your work
              
      
              
        Create a page to begin. Choose a blank page or a starter flow. Manage
        pages lets you rename, duplicate, reorder, and delete pages. Deleting
        the last page leaves 0 pages created; Undo brings it back.
      
              
      
              
        Build a flow from a list creates up to 100 connected steps in one
        undoable action. Add the next step connects a new step automatically.
        Read & export text includes every page in a downloadable outline.
      
              
      
              
        Check diagram finds missing endpoints, ambiguous branch labels,
        disconnected steps, unreachable routes from the selected step, and
        loops. These are observations, not proof that a process is wrong.
      
              
      
              Current boundaries
              
      
              
        Basic steps, decisions, groups, directed connections, and multiple pages
        are supported. Images, custom shapes, edge decorations, and rich
        formatting are simplified in this preview. Connection navigation follows
        the file’s source-to-target direction. Use draw.io to inspect complex
        visual layouts.
      
              
      
              
        Files are limited to 8 MB, 40 pages, 2,000 cells per page, and 24 MB of
        combined expanded page data. This release has automated accessibility
        checks; it has not yet been validated with blind participants.
      
              
      
              
        To install, use your browser’s “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”
        option when available. HTTPS or localhost is required for offline mode.
